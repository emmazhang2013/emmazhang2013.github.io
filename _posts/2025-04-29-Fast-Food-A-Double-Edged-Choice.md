Nowadays, more and more people started to eat fast food. It can cause many problems, but it doesn't mean that it has no pros. 

Firstly, fast food like hamburgers and fried chicken can cause obesity problems: the high sugar, salt and other things can only make you fatter and fatter. And it may cause mental problems. Imagine: if you are holding a bucket of fried chicken, what do you want to do? Sit in front of a TV and watch! That why you're becoming lazier.

Secondly, it price attracts many people. It's very cheap. Well, you have to admit that this is a good thing about fast food. Maybe some people think fast food is convenient and cheap, so they'd choose to eat fast food again and again.

Thirdly, as the "take-away" food becomes more and more popular, the fast food companies such as McDonald's and the KFC delivers the food in a short period of time. And that made fast food more convenient. So this is another good thing about fast food. It arrives your house as quick as you can imagine.

To sum up, fast food isn't always a bad thing — but it's not a good thing, either. What we need to do is to know that fast food has the pros and cons, and choose to eat it wisely.
