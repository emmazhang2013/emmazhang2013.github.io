Recently, I used a good website where I can download music. It's called "In love with music", and it's very convenient to use. But the website isn't just convenient.

"In love with music" is very convenient and easy to use. When you open the website, it will pop out a note to teach you to use it — it has many uses! It's cheap, too. If you are a "VIP", you can use it for free! And downloading music from this website is really fast! You just have to click "download", and it's done!

This website also has lots of other uses and features. For example, you can change the sizes of the words in it, which is really convenient for people who has bad eyesite. Also, it can know your favorite types of music, so it can recommend other songs like the ones I'm in love with. But the most amazing part of this website is that you can make music by yourself in this website! You can choose to play the piano, guitar or other musical instruments.

I'd recommend this website to anyone who wants to fall in love with music and likes music a lot, especially the people who has bad eyesite, old people, and young people who are very busy. 

This is an interesting and easy-to-use website called "In love with music". And it has got some special uses!
