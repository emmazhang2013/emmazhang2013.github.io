It is a phenomenon that nowadays people wastes so much resources and throw away too much things. Well, I also agree with it. So it's very important to know how to change this phenomenon.

First, I want to talk about wasting food. It's not good! When people buy ingredients in the market or eat in a restaurant, they'd always buy more than they eat. And then, when they finished eating, they throw away the food. It's not right because these are food! Sometimes they can save lives! When people throw away the food, always remember there are many poor people on earth who can't afford as much food as they need.

Second, I want to talk about throwing away old clothes. Well, if people cannot wear them anymore, they can always give it to people in need. For example, if the clothes aren't fit for your children anymore, you can give it to other people who have younger children, or give it to the poor. And the fabric of the clothes can be reused to make new clothes.

And at last, I want to talk about the "white trash": plastic bags. It is useful and popular, but it takes about 100 years to go into the ground and soil. So I suggest everyone to use fabric bags during shopping.

In a nutshell, I think we shouldn't waste too much because we just have one earth. There are no more resources if we use and waste them all.
