Most people think imagination is very important in life. I agree with it, but I think sometimes we should use it carefully. Today, let's discuss about "Imaginations".

I think imagination is a good "skill" and it's very important in life. I read a book called "Anne of Green Gables", and the main character Anne is very imaginative. She solved lots of problems using her imagination, so I think it is very important and useful in life.

Having lots of ideas can help a lot, too. I was in a science club before, and our task was making a useful mooncar. I had lots of ideas (which means I was creative), so it helps a lot in deciding  the mooncar's uses. You see, being creative is also very important in life.

But sometimes, we should be careful in using our imagination. Once, the little girl Anne has to get through the woods at night. She was too imaginative, so she thought there must be some ghosts in there. Then, she was too scared to get through. Being imaginative is quite important and useful, but sometimes we should use it on the right place. 

Today, we discussed about the imagination. It's good to be imaginative and creative, but we should use our imagination carefully and wisely.
