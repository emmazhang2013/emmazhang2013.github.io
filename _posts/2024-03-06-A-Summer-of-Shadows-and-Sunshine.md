Yesterday, I read *The Book Thief* page 138 to 153, today, I'm writing about it.

Part one is about a miserable jew, which had been caught, and thrown into jail. He wanted to go out, but never succeed. Part two is about the issues happening in Himmel Street, also a short review of the summer, 1940. In this summer, many things happened.

	1. Liesel read The Should Shrug every night.
	2. When Liesel have to pick up the washing, she'll read in the mayor's library.
	3. They play soccer everyday on Himmel Street.
	4. Liesel and Rudy are starving, so they decided to join a club that steal apples from a nearby farm.
	5. Swimming in Amber River, one time per week.

This is really a fantastic summer vacation, isn't it?

The jewish people had been treated very bad by the Nazi party, and Max was one of them which is very unfair! I'll never complain about the Nazi party, just like what Hagrid in *Harry Potter* said: "Everything which had to come will come, and if it came, we'll not complain and just do it."
