I read a wonderful book called The Book Thief. Its author is Markus Zusak. It's a novel. Today, I'd like to talk about the main topic, central idea and things we can learn from the book.

Firstly, I'd like to tell you what is the story about. It happened in Munich, Germany, during the Second World War. The main character is a teenage girl called Liesel, and a few of her friends and people from her foster family. The book describes Liesel's life in Munich. She met a Jew, and became friends with him, so her foster parents had to hide him in the basement. It's thrilling! And Liesel said "Heil Hitler" when she should, but when she came home, she would talk with her 24 year-old Jewish friend.

Secondly, the central idea should be revealed. We already know the story is about the teenage years a girl spent in a foster family during the war time. Liesel is covered with love. Her foster family loves her, her friends love her, and her teacher loves her too. So I think the central idea is "friendship and love always exists, even in times that the war happens".

Thirdly, I want to talk about what I learned from this book: We should always love the world and the people around us, so the world will be much better and happier. And care about now, catch every moments full of joy, because this happiness won't last forever. The Book Thief is written for the author's parents, because they grew up in the war time.

In the nutshell, The Book Thief is a book with a main topic, a central idea and we can learn something from it. So it's an interesting and meaningful book to read.
