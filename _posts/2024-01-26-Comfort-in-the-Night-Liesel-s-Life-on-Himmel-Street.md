This week, I read “The Book Thief” page 36 to 68, today I’m writing a review of it.

These pages is mainly about Lisel’s life at Himmel Street, where she lived with her frost parents: Hans and Rosa Humbermann. Every night, when Lisel nightmare, her frost Papa, Hans, would come to Lisel’s room and talk to her. And every day, Lisel will get out to play soccer with other kids in Himmel Street, she even made friend with a boy called Rudy(great lover of Jesse Owens) who loves he a lot!

When I read the pages of 62 to 68, I thought Hans Hambermann is a very good Papa. Every night Lisel nightmare, he would go to her room and calm her down, when she wet the bed, he will wash the sheets! About the “midnight class”, I think it’s a brilliant idea to produce that, especially for the method to learn. It’s really a nice Papa, isn’t he?

This is the review, I hope you like to read “The Book Thief” too!
