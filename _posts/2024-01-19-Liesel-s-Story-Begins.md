Yesterday, I read 《The Book Thief》page 19 to 35, so today I’m writing a review of it.

The got off the train to bury the little brother. Then, Lisel found a book in the snow. She took it. This is why Lisel was called “The boot thief”. Mama is angry for almost all the time, and often do something Lisel couldn’t imagine of. Papa is kind, usually had an new idea when Lisel is stucked. I wish I had parents like this!
