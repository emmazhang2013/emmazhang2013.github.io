Yesterday, I read *The Book Thief* page 168 to 186, so today I'm writing a review of it.

The first part is about a Jew named Max went to the Hubermann's house. His father taught Hans to play accordion, so Max has the key. The second part is about Hans Hubermann, and his secret life. He escaped three times from the Death. He was saved by the Jew, so he will not join the Nazis. He has two children. The third part is about Liesel being "the good girl" who never speak in publish, do something bad or get her clothes dirty.

I think the second part is insane! I can't imagine such a good man like him had been like that! He almost fought with Mr. Herbert, and painted his door white! The third part is mesmerizing. What does the writer wanted to tell us by the title "A Good Girl" and the issues about Max Vadenburg and Hans's accordion? I think the things are more difficult than when Liesel came alone to the Hubermann's house. Maybe this would be the start of the "storm".

So, what next?
