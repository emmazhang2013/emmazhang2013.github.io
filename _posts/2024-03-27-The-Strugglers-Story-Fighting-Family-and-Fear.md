Last week, I read *The Book Thief* page 187 to 206, today I'm writing a review of it.

The first part is Max Vadenburg's childhood and his life. He is the struggler, and he was a jew that  has been captured by the German Nazis. Max is good at fist fighting as he always fight with his best friend ever: Walter. He had a fantastic life with his family, friends and enemy. The second part is about Rosa Hubermann's infamous pea soup, drank by Max. He vomits a lot by drinking that. The third part is about papa and Liesel's conversation. Papa said he will burn all of Liesel's books. He said the Fuhrer is coming.

I think Max is a man with story. He watched more, learned more and experienced more than other 24 years old men. He is a man with heart, with eyes with care and emotions. By reading the third part, I realized the bombs are coming. Horrible life are coming. I knew this story wasn't like The Snow Queen. In fairy tales like that, the problems are solved as quick as can. *The Book Thief* was like *Anne's Diary*, they're all about fire. The coming fire.

The first bomb will arriver later.
