There is always an argument about whether life was better in the past or better now. I think the debate is reasonable. Today let's discuss about life 100 years ago and compare it with nowaday life.

Health is one of the most important thing and base to live. You have to admit, healthcare is way better nowadays. The hospital service is efficient and clean; the doctors are professional and reliable; and the medicine are really easy to buy online.

Another point to consider, though, is the quality of life. And that means entertainments and free-time activities. In the past, people can't watch TV or play with their phones—they weren't invented yet. The men were playing card games and the women were busy doing housework and taking care of their children—they don't even have free-times! But now, we can watch TV, play computer games, play board games, and so on. It's rather interesting and eye-catching.

However, nowadays life can have lots of potential harms and can cause many problems: watching screens for too long may cause eyesight problems;  the number of people who'd prefer watching TV rather than doing sports during weekends are increasing. The pace of life is too fast! But in the past, people can spend hours and hours reading or enjoying a cup of tea during afternoons—it's relaxing, really.

Well, in some cases, life now is better than life in the past. But sometimes, life in the past can be better. In spite of everything, I think next time when you want to compare a few things, do remember to look things in a diversity of perspectives.
