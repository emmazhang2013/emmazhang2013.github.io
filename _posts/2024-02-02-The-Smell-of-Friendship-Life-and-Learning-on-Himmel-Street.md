Yesterday, I read *The Book Thief* page 68 to 80. And this is another short review of it.

The first title “The smell of friendship” is really attractive! How can a friendship been smelled？What smell would friendship be? Actually, it’s the smell of Liesel’s Papa number two (he foster parents), and is filled with cigarette and sweat. What a fun title!

These pages are mainly about some fun things which is happening in Himmel Street, and Liesel’s school life. It’s hard, and sister Maria often call somebody to the corridor. Two times a hour. That’s awful.

The first part, 68 to 72, I think the discribe for Papa’s face is vivid, accurate, serious, and even dignified and stateliness. That sounds like a picture that I can see. For the second part, I’m confused of why would Liesel read *The Grave Digger’s Handbook*, not the book that sister Maria wanted her to read? School life was difficult, as Liesel both love and hate it, both learn and fight there. This is the process of growing up!

And that’s why I love this book.
