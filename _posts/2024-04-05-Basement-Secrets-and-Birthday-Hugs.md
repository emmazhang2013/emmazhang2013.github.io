Yesterday I read *The Book Thief* page 207 to 238, so today I'm writing a review of it.

The first part is about daily life in Molching, the only difference is Frau Hubermann's basement. A Jew is hiding there. The Jew Max and Liesel became friends when they found something common between them. They all dreamed nightmares. In Liesel's birthday, Max had nothing to give, but Liesel hugged him and said thank you to him. The second part is the pages Max has drawn for Liesel as her late birthday gift. For Liesel, Max painted his favorite book white to draw on it. The third part is a introduction of chapter five. Rudy and Liesel did something. What did they do? I'm really curious about that and I really want to read the next part to find out.

I think it's really kind of the Hubermanns to let Max stay in their basement. In world war two, it's extremely dangerous to do that. For the second part, I think Max and Liesel's friendship is heating by the gift about the "standoverman". For the third part, I like the design of the "introduction" before each chapter. It could help us know what are we reading next. These are three adventurous, delightful parts.
