Nowdays, it is a phenomenon that people—especially teenagers, are worrying too much about the way they look. Well, is that a bad thing? Is doing beauty important or it's just a waste of time and money? Should they focus more on becoming interesting?

Firstly, I must admit that worrying about the way they look isn't the bad thing. Researches show that people are more likely to talk to strangers who looks healthy and beautiful, or wearing a certain color of clothes. But that's just something called "first impression". However, after the first time they meet each other, people would care more about the person's "in", which contains whether he or she is interesting.

Another point to consider, going to salons and doing beauty costs a lot of money. I'm not saying that it's not important, but we shouldn't make the place to be a "money-burner".

However, sometimes the first impressions people give is important too. Friendship is based on the first time they meet and talk, and all that are led to choosing "the one" in loads of strangers. Also, keeping "your beauty" is particularly vital for people who works in a fashional companies.
