Have you ever go to school trips? Well, it is a popular activity for schools nowadays. But there are debates about places children can go. Cities or countrysides? They all have their pros and cons.

Firstly, if you are going to organise a school trip, what will you think first? Of course you will think about interesting places to visit. Countrysides have better views, fresh air and nature! Cities have
libraries. It sounds boring but you can learn lots of things, such as science, history, art, and so on.

Secondly, you will think of transport. If you want to go to countrysides, you are going to organise buses, or even trains to move children. That also causes huge financial outlays. But if you want to stay in cities, well, bikes are convenient and available.

Thirdly, experiences are important, too. Living in cities are actually boring: you are stuck in all those schoolworks, courses or projects, and an important part of life is to relax. Gool off a little bit. Go to countrysides and return to the nature. Experience life and things we learned at school or work but never tried before. Also, you can learn something new here, like planting trees or taking care of animals. Open up yourself and see a wider world!

In the nutshell, I think cities and countrysides are both good for school trips: they have their own pros and cons. But I'd prefer going to countrysides if I had a choice.
