What is your first thought if I ask you: "Why do we work?" Well, most people must have thought about earning money. Today, we'll discuss about the connection between earning money and working, and finding other possibilities that would make your "working life" more interesting.

It seems as if "training and studies" and "working" are exactly different things, but actually as we work in companies, we are learning about working in groups, or training our interpersonal skills, which are really important in the rest of our life.

Also, if you think that learning all those skills are getting "work experiences", getting along with your colleagues are teaching you "life experiences". Yes, bonding with these people you see even day are definitely socializing. These are all better reasons to work.

Another point to consider, the feelings you feel when you work are also vital. Earning money is significant, but you have to care about yourself, no matter the physical health or mental health. Actually, there are jobs that can entertain yourself and earn money(that's the best jobs in my opinion), such as an author, an artist or a musician. But I believe, if you love your job and can do it with all your heart, you can learn the life experiences which are more important than just suffering to earn money.

All in all, working can be more interesting than you thought it would be. You can learn vital things from it, apart from working unhappily just to earn money.
