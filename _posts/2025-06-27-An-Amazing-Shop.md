Recently, a shop  with the name of "God Goods Convenient Shop" opened in my area. Today, I'm going to tell you about the shop and recommend it to you.

This shop is actually a supermarket, because it is quite big, and the goods are amazing. The goods their contains all you need for daily life: food, water, clothes, books, daily supplies, and even some toys are available to buy. But the most amazing and exciting part of this shop is its "subject room". Well, it's a room that shows goods that are changing every month. For example, summer is coming, and July is definitely a great month to go swimming, so the room shows swimming suits, swimming glasses, suncream and even the towels are half price. Well, the subject every month depends on the season and the weather, which is a great thing. This shop is located in our community centre yard, just beside of a school, and the children can get here to buy lunch.

Also, the staff here are so friendly. They are some helpful guides who would kindly ask you your needs and recommend goods for you. Teenagers can get what they want too: sports clothes, video games and basketballs, sandwiches, ice-creams and coffee. What do you want more? (Actually, this shop is disabled-free and elderly free, so maybe you'll see lots of old people and disabled people with guide dogs here.)

In the nutshell, I'm so glad to know and shop in this shop. And I must recommend that shop to people in all ages. I must admit, I'm deeply impressed by its functions, goods, the special "subject room" and the friendly staff there.
