I'm a student studying grade four in Friendship primary school. We have the best teacher on earth—Ms. Li.
"Ms. Li's birthday is next week!" Liz gasped when she turned the calendar into a new page in our classroom, "We should do something for her." "What about we throw her a birthday party?" I suggested. "A what?" Liz looked surprised. "A real birthday party! A brilliant surprise. It should contain gifts, cards, balloons and most importantly, a cake." I said. Liz smiled: "Great. But I recommend that we tell our classmates about that. They can help."

So, after a class meeting without the teacher, we discussed about what a wonderful birthday surprise party should be. Then I gave tasks to everyone. Me and Liz got the most significant jobs: buy gift—wrappers and choose a cake.

Finally it's Sunday. Liz bought the bows and I got the wrappers. When it was at night, I lied on the bed and imagined the party tomorrow. Ms. Li will be so surprised! The paintings and the balloons will be so perfect! Ms. Li will slice the cake. The cake will be so delicious……

Wait! The cake! Me and Liz completely forgot the birthday cake. I nearly jumped off my bed, and phoned Liz. We were so anxious. It was too late to call the bakery. When we were thinking hard, my grandma came.

"What the matter, sweetheart? What? You forgot the cake? Grandma's on it." She rushed to the kitchen. We all felt relieved.

Monday's coming! But I didn't feel well. Grandma sighed: "You caught a cold, sweetheart. Now you must stay at home, even though it's your teacher's birthday today." I cried with disappointment. Just as I grabbed the phone to call Liz to pick up the cake, the door bell rang. It was Ms. Li! "I heard that you are sick, so I stopped by to see you." She said gently. Then to all of our surprise, the door opened, and then……

All of my classmates came in. Some held the gifts, some grabbed the balloons, some took the birthday hat……
"Surprise!"

Ms. Li hugged us. Tears of joy ran down her cheek. Then we ate the cake and spent all day in my house.

Don't you think this is a wonderful birthday surprise party?
