This week, I read *The Book Thief* page 83 to 90. Today, I'm writing a review for it.

"A GIRL MADE OF DARKNESS", what does this title means? And, what happened to Liesel if she is made of DARKNESS? This made me read it urgely. But, actually it's just talking about Liesel had stolen another book: The Shoulder Shrug. Why would the author write such a title like this? It's confusing!

"THE JOY OF CIGARETTE" is mainly about daily life at Himmel Street, and Liesel's first christmas with her foster parents and their sons. Papa gave Liesel a book or two, the money for books is from selling cigarette.

For the first part, I think this is a start for a new big PART, it's just like an Introduce of the story. Liesel had stolen her second book. People died more oftenly because of the war. A great sadness starting by joy. Every information by themselfes. This is a strange part. For the second part, I think the writer uses soft, warm words to describe the joy that sixteen cigarette could bring. Papa and mama are so good, although there are more "saumensch", "saukerl", "arschloch" in the room. But, this is how Rosa call someone she loved!

This is really strange, isn't it?
